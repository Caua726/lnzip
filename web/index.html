Paste a link here to read where it goes before you open it. Nothing is looked up and nothing is sent anywhere — the answer is already inside the code.

Points to

A URL made mostly of a random identifier cannot be compressed by anything — a YouTube video id is 66 bits of pure randomness, and no amount of modelling removes randomness. lnzip wins on URLs built from words and structure, and wins hugest on the long ones buried in utm_source and tracking parameters.

What it gives up in exchange for storing nothing: a code is only as short as the URL is predictable, and it can be longer than what a shortener with a database would hand you. What it gives back is that there is no database to leak, expire, or be subpoenaed.
